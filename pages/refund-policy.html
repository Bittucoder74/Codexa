Refund & Cancellation Policy

Plain-language rules for what happens if you need to cancel, transfer, or request a refund. Last updated August 28, 2026.

The Short Version

Before you read all 19 sections

Here's the part most people actually want to know. The full policy below is what legally governs your enrollment — read it too.

7 days to ask, not a guarantee

You can request a cancellation or refund within 7 days of enrollment. It gets reviewed — it isn't automatically approved.

Non-refundable after 7 days

Past the 7-day window, fees are generally non-refundable — even if you haven't attended a single class.

Course transfer within 1 month

Want a different course instead? You can request a transfer within a month of enrolling, subject to seat availability.

If we discontinue a course

Our fault, not yours: 100% refund if within your first 3 months, 60% refund after — see Section 12 for the full terms.

At Codexa Tech Academy, we are committed to providing quality education, practical training, and a supportive learning environment. We understand that circumstances may change after enrollment. This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the conditions applicable to course fees, enrollment cancellation, course transfers, batch changes, and refunds.

By enrolling in any course at Codexa Tech Academy, the student and/or parent/guardian acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to this Refund & Cancellation Policy, along with the applicable Terms & Conditions of the Academy.

Codexa Tech Academy does not provide an automatic or unconditional refund for course enrollment. Any request for cancellation or refund will be reviewed based on the circumstances of the request, the time elapsed since enrollment, the course status, payments made, and the services or learning resources already provided. Where appropriate, the Academy Management may consider and approve a full or partial refund at its discretion, subject to this policy and applicable law.

A student or parent/guardian may contact Codexa Tech Academy regarding cancellation or refund within 7 calendar days from the date of enrollment/payment. However, submitting a request within these 7 days does not automatically guarantee a refund. The Academy Management will review the request and may approve, reject, or determine an appropriate refund or adjustment based on the circumstances. If classes, course materials, resources, assessments, projects, mentoring, or other services have already been provided, these may be considered while determining any refund amount.

After 7 calendar days from the date of enrollment/payment, course fees will generally be non-refundable. This applies even if the student has attended only a few classes, has not attended any classes, has decided not to continue the course, has changed their personal, academic, or professional plans, or is unable or unwilling to continue the course. However, in exceptional and genuine circumstances, the Academy Management may review an individual request and decide whether any refund, adjustment, transfer, or alternative solution is appropriate.

Any registration, admission, enrollment, or administrative fee, where separately charged, is non-refundable. Such fees will not normally be included in any approved course-fee refund, except where otherwise required by applicable law or specifically approved by the Academy in writing.

Students who enroll under an installment payment arrangement must follow the payment schedule communicated at the time of enrollment. Payment through installments does not create a separate right to cancel the course or receive a refund. After the applicable refund period has expired, amounts already paid will generally not be refundable, even if the student decides not to continue the course.

A student may request to transfer from one Codexa course to another course. A course-change request must normally be submitted within 1 month from the date of enrollment. Course transfer is not automatic and will depend on availability of the requested course, available seats, batch schedule, course duration and structure, difference in course fees, and approval from the Academy Management. If the new course has a higher fee, the student may be required to pay the applicable difference. If the new course has a lower fee, any adjustment or credit will be determined by the Academy Management. Course transfer does not automatically entitle the student to a cash refund.

Students may request a change of batch due to genuine academic, professional, or personal circumstances. Batch changes are subject to prior discussion and approval by Codexa Tech Academy. A batch change will only be possible if the Academy has a suitable alternative batch and available seat. The Academy reserves the right to decline a batch-change request if a suitable alternative batch is not available.

Codexa Tech Academy may provide training through online and/or offline modes, depending on the course, batch, schedule, and availability. If a course is initially conducted online and an offline batch becomes available later, the Academy may inform students about the availability of the offline option. Transfer from online to offline training will be subject to availability, schedule, seats, location, and Academy approval. The future availability of an offline batch does not automatically create a right to a refund or compensation.

Failure to attend classes does not, by itself, create an automatic right to a refund. This includes absence due to personal reasons, academic commitments, employment or work schedule, travel, change of plans, lack of interest, personal or technical difficulties, or any other reason attributable to the student.

Courses purchased under discounts, promotional offers, scholarship arrangements, early-bird offers, referral benefits, or other special pricing may be subject to specific refund or adjustment conditions. Where separate terms are communicated at the time of enrollment, those terms will apply to the relevant enrollment.

Codexa Tech Academy may provide students with course notes, PDFs and digital materials, assignments, project resources, recorded content where applicable, software-related resources, and other educational resources. Once such resources have been provided or access has been granted, they may be considered when reviewing any exceptional refund request. Access to learning resources does not create an independent right to a refund.

If Codexa Tech Academy is unable to continue and permanently discontinues a course or batch due to circumstances attributable to the Academy, the following refund arrangement will generally apply:

Before completion of 3 months: if the Academy permanently discontinues the relevant course before completion of 3 months from the student's course start date, the Academy will generally provide a 100% refund of the course fees actually paid by the student for that course.

After completion of 3 months: if the Academy permanently discontinues the relevant course after completion of 3 months from the student's course start date, the Academy will generally provide a refund of 60% of the course fees actually paid by the student.

This provision applies where the Academy is unable to provide a reasonable alternative arrangement. Where reasonably possible, Codexa Tech Academy may first offer an alternative batch, schedule, course mode, or suitable replacement arrangement.

In exceptional circumstances, the Academy Management may review an individual case and, at its discretion, approve a full refund, partial refund, course transfer, batch transfer, fee adjustment, or another reasonable solution. Approval of an exceptional case does not create a precedent or guarantee the same decision in any future case.

Where a refund is approved: the student or parent/guardian may be required to submit the necessary refund request and payment details; the Academy will verify the student's enrollment and payment records; the approved refund amount will be communicated to the applicant; the refund will normally be processed through the original payment method, where reasonably possible; and processing time may depend on the bank, payment gateway, or payment service provider. Codexa Tech Academy will make reasonable efforts to process an approved refund within 7–10 business days after completion of the required verification and documentation.

Refund or cancellation requests should preferably be submitted in writing through the Academy's official email address. The request should include the student's full name, parent/guardian's name where applicable, course name, enrollment date, contact number, payment details or receipt if available, and reason for the request. The Academy may request additional information or documents when necessary to review the request.

Students are expected to make their enrollment decision after considering the course syllabus, duration, fees, class schedule, training mode, and other relevant course details. Once the applicable refund period has expired, a student's decision to stop attending or participating in the course will generally not create a refund obligation for Codexa Tech Academy.

Codexa Tech Academy reserves the right to update, modify, or revise this Refund & Cancellation Policy when necessary. Any updated version will be published on the official Codexa Tech Academy website with a revised "Last Updated" date. The policy applicable to an enrollment will generally be the version communicated and accepted at the time of that enrollment, subject to applicable law.

Before completing enrollment, students and parents/guardians are advised to carefully review course details, duration, fees, payment terms, class schedule, training mode, Refund & Cancellation Policy, and other applicable Academy policies. By completing enrollment and payment, the student and/or parent/guardian confirms that they have had an opportunity to review and understand the applicable terms.
