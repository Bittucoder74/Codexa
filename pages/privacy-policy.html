Codexa Tech Academy ("Codexa," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or enroll in a course, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

By using our website or enrolling in a course, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information you give us directly: full name, email address, phone number, date of birth or age range where relevant, highest qualification, and any information you type into our admission, contact, or counselling forms.

Payment information: when you pay for a course, payment is processed by our third-party payment gateway, [Payment Gateway Name]. We receive confirmation of payment and limited transaction details; we do not store your full card, UPI, or bank details on our own servers.

Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent on our website, collected through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 4).

Communication records: messages you send us via WhatsApp, email, phone, or our contact form, and records of calls or chats with our admissions and support team.

We use the information we collect to: process your enrollment and payments; provide course access, live classes, mentoring, and placement support; respond to your questions and support requests; send you enrollment confirmations, class schedules, and important updates about your course; send you promotional messages about new batches or courses, where you have not opted out; improve our website, courses, and teaching; and comply with legal, accounting, and regulatory requirements.

We process your personal data on the basis of: your consent (for example, when you submit an admission or contact form); the performance of a contract (to deliver the course you've enrolled in); and our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Academy, where these interests do not override your rights. Where required by applicable law, we will seek your explicit consent before processing sensitive information.

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to remember your preferences (such as light/dark mode), understand how visitors use our site, and measure the effectiveness of our content. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; some parts of the website may not function as intended if you do.

We share information only where necessary to run the Academy, with: our payment gateway ([Payment Gateway Name]), to process course fees; communication tools (WhatsApp Business, email, and SMS providers), to send you updates and respond to queries; website analytics and hosting providers, to keep the site running and understand how it's used; and law enforcement or regulators, where required by law. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Your data is primarily stored [on servers located in India / with our hosting and form-processing providers — confirm actual storage location(s) here].

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain academic and financial records, comply with legal and tax obligations, and resolve disputes. If you contact us but do not enroll, we may retain your contact details for a reasonable period to follow up, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to: access the personal information we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information; request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal and record-keeping obligations; withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time; and lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12.

Some of our students may be under 18. Where a student is a minor, we require that enrollment and any related consent be provided or confirmed by a parent or legal guardian. Parents/guardians may contact us at any time to review, correct, or request deletion of their child's personal information.

We may send you information about new batches, courses, or offers via email, SMS, or WhatsApp. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying "STOP," unsubscribing via the link in the message, or emailing us directly. You will still receive essential, non-marketing communications related to a course you've enrolled in (such as class schedules or payment receipts).

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
